Frequently asked questions.

General information about the NewbieMail collection and digital experience.

What is NewbieMail?

NewbieMail is a modern eyewear brand concept focused on sunglasses, optical-inspired frames, blue-light styles, and everyday design.

Can I purchase frames on this site?

The current site presents the NewbieMail collection and brand experience. Ordering and payment options will only appear when official commerce services are available.

Are prescription lenses available?

No prescription service is represented on this site at this time. Product and lens options should always be confirmed on an official product page before ordering.

Where can I find shipping and return information?

Shipping and return policies will be published when those services are active and can be stated accurately.

How do I hear about new frames?

New releases and collection updates will be published on the New Arrivals and Collections pages.
