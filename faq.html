Help

Frequently asked questions.

Does NewbieMail sell these products directly?

No. Product buttons open the original retailer listing where availability, checkout, shipping, and returns are handled.

Are the prices live?

They are reference prices observed on the cited retailer pages. Color and size variants can have different prices, and the retailer may update them at any time.

Where do the product images come from?

The designer edit loads public product imagery from the original retailer's CDN. The product source page is linked from every detail page.

Is NewbieMail affiliated with the brands or Designer Eyes?

No affiliation is implied. Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

How does Saved Frames work?

Saved products are stored locally in your browser using localStorage and are not uploaded to a server.
